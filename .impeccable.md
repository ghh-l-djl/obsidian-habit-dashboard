## Design Context

### Users
Obsidian desktop and mobile users who manage daily events, habits, tasks, logs, and plans from one at-a-glance dashboard. The primary job is to review and update the day quickly without leaving the vault.

### Brand Personality
Calm, focused, and warm. The interface should feel like a quiet personal workspace rather than a dense productivity console.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined, warm minimal interface built around the existing cream, brown, and muted pastel palette. Preserve the current light visual weight, compact controls, and compatibility with Obsidian themes on desktop and mobile.

### Design Principles
- Keep the day's most actionable information visible at a glance.
- Let dashboard sections use available space instead of leaving accidental gaps.
- Preserve clear hierarchy with restrained borders, color, and decoration.
- Adapt layout behavior to desktop and mobile rather than merely shrinking it.
- Maintain accessible targets, readable contrast, and predictable interaction states.
